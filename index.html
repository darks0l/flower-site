Product thesis

Sports markets without sportsbook clutter.

flower should feel premium, social, and immediate. The product language stays simple: what game matters, what market is moving, and where should a user go next without getting buried in noise?

League-first structure

NFL, NBA, MLB, and college football should each feel like a real home, not just a filter.

Game pages that convert

Each matchup page should organize the full stack: headline question, related markets, movement, and personal follow state.

Account-first onboarding

Familiar identity rails make entry feel instant before deeper product habits take over.
